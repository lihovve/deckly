Shapes in the editor

Shapes · New card · question

15 outline shapes. A slider controls outline width independently of the pen; no fill.

Placement · New card · question

A star is selected. Tap the sheet with the pen to place it; the cross cancels insertion.

Selection · New card · question

Drag near the outline to move. Corners resize; the handle above the frame rotates. The hollow interior stays free for writing.

Moving · New card · question

The shape has moved within the sheet. Its size and outline width are unchanged.

Resizing · New card · question

Width and height change independently; stretching does not thicken the outline.

After placement · New card · question

The selection frame is hidden and the normal toolbar returns. Tap near the outline to select the shape again; the interior stays free for writing.

Arrow direction · New card · question

Drag either endpoint to change the length and direction. Drag the shaft to move the whole arrow. Outline width and arrowhead size stay constant.

Rotation · New card · question

Drag the handle above the frame to rotate the shape. Corner handles resize along its rotated axes. Outline width stays constant.
